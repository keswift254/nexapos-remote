# NexaPOS 1.0.93

**Reports are gone for a cashier.** The owner asked for the Reports section to be removed from the cashier user completely: a cashier has no Reports button, and the app sends a cashier who asks for Reports (a link, an old web bookmark) back to the dashboard. The owner and managers keep Reports as before. (A cashier still prints the receipt at the end of each sale; reprinting an older receipt is for the owner or a manager.)

**New Sale stands out and is always at hand.** On every dashboard, New Sale is the first thing under your name: a big raised button (64 pixels tall, large bold text). On a PC it stands on its own at the left with Inventory, Categories and Expenses smaller at the right; on a phone it is full width with those buttons under it, above the figures, so nobody scrolls to start a sale. F2 still opens a new sale from the keyboard.

The NexaPOS Lite till (build 2026-11-23.3) does the same: no Reports for a cashier, and New Sale big and first on its dashboard.
