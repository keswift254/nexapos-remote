# NexaPOS 1.0.84

A better "Last 7 Days" chart on the dashboard, in the Android, Windows and web versions. Selling, stock, refunds, licences and payments are not changed.

- **Tap a day to see its figures.** Tap anywhere in a day's column on the Last 7 Days chart and that day's date, Sales, Expenses and Net Profit are written under the chart; the other days fade so the one you chose stands out. Before, you had to tap exactly on one thin bar and only that bar's figure popped up.
- **Figures stay private.** When the dashboard is hiding its money figures, the chosen day shows stars instead of amounts.
