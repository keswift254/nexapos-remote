# NexaPOS 1.0.21

- Places thermal printing and New Sale side by side on the receipt screen.
- Refreshes sale receipts with a bolder business header, support footer, and barcode.
- Adds matching barcode/footer design to daily and monthly report PDFs.
- Prevents duplicate report print jobs while a report is already being sent to the printer.
- Hides today's sales and profit by default, with a 60-second reveal control.
- Adds an encrypted manual backup command under the administrator Settings menu.
- Sorts the online device dashboard with the newest registrations first.
