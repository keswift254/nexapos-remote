# NexaPOS 1.0.70

The Support conversation now works like a messaging app. Install it on every device; nothing about selling, stock, licences or payments changes.

- **Your messages are blue.** Your shop's messages are blue, NexaPOS Support's are grey.
- **Delivered.** The moment you send, the message appears with "Sending…" under it; when NexaPOS Support has it, it says **Delivered** and plays a short send sound. If it could not be sent it says "Not delivered. Tap to try again" - tap it to send it again.
- **Swipe to see the time.** Drag the conversation to the left (or scroll sideways on a trackpad) and the time each message was sent slides into view; let go and it slides back. Each day has its own heading.
- **Double-tap a message.** A menu opens with six reactions (heart, thumbs up, thumbs down, laughing, !!, ?), **Reply** and **Copy text**. A reaction appears under the message for both sides - tap it to take yours off. **Reply** quotes the message above your reply; tap a quote to jump to the message it came from. (Long-press opens the same menu.)
- **New messages arrive by themselves.** An open conversation checks for replies and reactions every 20 seconds, and always opens on the newest message.

Support's side - the dashboard that answers your tickets, and the emails about them - gets the same replies and reactions; that part is already live and needs no update.

This release does not change the licence, payment or database format. Windows 7/8 keeps its separate installer.
