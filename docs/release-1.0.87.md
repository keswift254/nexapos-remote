# NexaPOS 1.0.87

**Settings looks like NexaPOS Lite's.** The gear on the dashboard now opens a Settings page with the same pages as before, each on its own rounded row with an arrow, the way the Lite till shows its Settings. On a wide screen the rows stay in one column in the middle.

The pages, their order and who can open them are unchanged: everyone sees Security & Privacy, My License and Software Update; administrators also see Business, Receipts & Themes, Payments & Payouts, M-Pesa Till Phone, Sync & Invites, Devices in This Shop, Backup & Restore and Date, Time & Region.

Nothing else changes.
