# NexaPOS 1.0.92

**A cashier no longer sees the totals or the profit in Reports.** After 1.0.91 took the shop's money off a cashier's dashboard, the owner asked for the same in Reports. A cashier's Reports now lists the sales and refunds of the day, month or period they pick, each with its own amount, so they can still open a receipt to reprint it or refund it. Everything that adds them up is shown only to the owner (Admin) and managers: Paid Sales, Total Cash Received, Total Paid by M-Pesa, Total Expenses, Gross and Net Profit, Grand Total, Transactions, the Total Paid Sales and Total Refunds lines, the Expenses section and tab, and Print and Export PDF (the printed report carries the totals).

The NexaPOS Lite till (build 2026-11-23.2) does the same in its Reports and hides the "Today on this iPad" totals on its Sales tab from a cashier.
