# NexaPOS 1.0.78

Hold a sale while you serve someone else, and quick sign in with Face ID or a fingerprint on the web version. Stock, reports, payments and licences are not affected.

- **Hold a sale.** In the cart there is now a **Hold** button next to Complete Sale. Tap it (give the sale a name like "Mama Njeri" if you wish) and the sale is set aside, so you can serve the next customer straight away.
- **Come back to it later.** The **Held sales** button at the top of the New Sale and Cart screens shows how many sales are waiting. Open one to carry on and complete it, or throw it away. If you already have something in the cart, opening a held sale holds the current one first, so nothing is ever lost.
- **What holding does and does not do.** A held sale is only a saved cart: it does not take stock and records no payment until you complete it. Held sales stay on the device they were made on (they are not sent to your other devices), survive closing the app or restarting the device, and are cleared if the device is moved to a different shop.
- **Face ID / fingerprint on the web version.** In the web version, open Settings > Security & Privacy and turn on Quick sign in. Your phone, iPad, Mac or PC then asks for your face or fingerprint when you press the quick sign in button on the sign-in screen. Your NexaPOS password is never stored. It is set up once per browser (an iPhone Home Screen copy is separate from Safari), needs a secure (https) address, and your password still works as before.
