# NexaPOS 1.0.91

**A cashier no longer sees the shop's money on the dashboard.** Sales Today, Net Profit Today, Stock Value and the Last 7 Days chart are shown only to the owner (Admin) and managers. A cashier's dashboard has Low Stock and the New Sale button, and nothing else to reveal: no stars, no eye, no transaction count. The owner and managers see everything as before, and the amounts someone showed with the eye are hidden again for the next person who signs in on the same screen.

Everything in 1.0.90 is included: a till stuck on "already recorded with different content" uploads again by itself, the dashboard no longer keeps refreshing while the till syncs, and on a PC the whole dashboard fits on one screen.
